Frankenstein by Shirley Jackson

Late to the bandwagon

Frankenstein is a classic horror book that has endless potential for analysis and interpretation.
Dr. Frankenstein and his experiments lead way for a story of murder, trials and a thrilling exploration of what separates humans from monsters. Because after all, what are we without love?

this book is for you if...

  • You are a fan of philosophy.
  • You want to learn more about the foundations of contemporary horror.
  • You constantly rant to your friends about the meaning of life.
  • You want to connect with human nature in a gothic ambiance.
  • You want to explore literary analyses of a everlasting classic.
