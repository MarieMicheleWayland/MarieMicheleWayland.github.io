Walkability & Pre-Car Design: Because Montréal developed into a major metropolis
before car-centric suburban sprawl took over, its central neighborhoods feature a dense, human-scale street grid,
historic architecture, and reliable public transit. Food, Arts & Festivals: The city is celebrated for its dynamic culinary scene
(from classic poutine to world-class fine dining), a packed calendar of international festivals,
and a unique blend of European charm and North American energy.
